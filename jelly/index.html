Jelly Town

You are a wobbly jelly person in a wobbly town. Do jobs to earn coins: deliver pizzas, collect the bins, move crates, drive the taxi. Throw beach balls, bounce, flop and dance. Fix the broken rocket to fly to the Moon, and look for the secret hidden in the mountain. Spend them on hats, colours, a pet and the biggest house in town.

Mouse (or left and right arrows): look   WASD: wobble about   Space: jump   E: use things, get in a car
In a car: W drive, S reverse, A and D (or left and right arrows) steer
F or click: pick up and throw   Q: flop over   G: dance

Look for the spinning gold stars: that's where the jobs and the shop are. Bounce on the trampolines in the park, and watch out for traffic! There are 12 presents hidden around the world.
