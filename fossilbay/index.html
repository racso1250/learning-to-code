Fossil Bay

Welcome to your very own dinosaur park. Fence in paddocks, hatch dinosaurs from eggs, and keep them fed and happy. Build paths, viewing decks and shops so visitors can come and see them. But be careful: an unhappy dinosaur will bash its fence down. If one gets out, jump in the ranger jeep and catch it!

Mouse: point and click to build. Hold and drag for paddocks and paths. Right click: stop building
W, A, S, D or arrow keys: move the camera   Q, E: turn it   Mouse wheel or Z, X: zoom
1 to 0: choose a tool   P: pause   O: fast forward
J: jump in the ranger jeep. Then W, A, S, D to drive and Space to fire a sleepy dart

Click a paddock with the Look tool to see how its dinosaurs feel and why. Dinosaurs get angry when they are hungry, lonely, cramped, or shut in with a meat-eater. Stronger fences hold for longer. Visitors only count dinosaurs they can see, so every paddock needs a viewing deck beside it that is joined to the gate by a path.
