A first-person space horror

Space Explorers

You and your crew are lost in space. By day, search each planet for fuel and a way home. But be back at the ship before dark. Something lives on these planets. It only comes out at night, and it is very good at hiding.

Mouse (or left and right arrows): look   WASD: move   Click or Space: fire   E: use the ship   N: start the night early
1: build a turret   2: build a wall   3: build a lamp   P: pause

At night you can only see what your torch points at. Demons hide in the dark: watch for their eyes. The small ones freeze when you look at them, and creep closer when you turn away. Light keeps them slow, so build lamps!
