Wildlings

Wind Isles

Sail your own little boat between eight windy islands. Catch and train 60 new Wildlings, win a Wind Chime from the Keeper of each island, then sail into the storm to become the Wind Champion.

W, A, S, D: walk, or steer your boat   Shift: run
Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look around   Mouse wheel: zoom
1, 2, 3, 4: your Wildling's moves   E: throw a capture orb   F: Starburst   Q: swap Wildling
Space: sail, hop out, talk, rest, gather wood and sell shells   B: buy orbs at a shop   H: build a home
M: map   O: outfit   X: team   Z: Wilddex   T: type chart

The wind dial shows which way the wind is blowing. Your boat is fast with the wind behind it and slow against it, and attacks that go the same way as the wind hit harder. Hop out of your boat anywhere the water is shallow. At night, giant sea Wildlings rise out of the ocean: stay close to a home you have built (6 wood from palm trees, then H) or you will be swept back to the start. Sea shells from the beaches sell for coins at the shops (blue roofs), and rest huts (green cross) heal your team.
