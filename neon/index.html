Wildlings

Neon City

One big 3D city, full of Wildlings. Design your own outfit, then explore. By day, catch wild ones in the parks. By night, trainers battle in the glowing Battle Zone. Beat the giant raid Wildlings in the purple portals to win rare ones. Fight in real time, dodge their attacks, and climb the Night League from Rank Z all the way to Rank A.

W, A, S, D: walk (your Wildling follows, so move to dodge!)   Shift: run
Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look around   Mouse wheel: zoom
1, 2, 3: attacks   E: throw a capture orb   F: Starburst (your Wildling changes into its Star form)   Q: swap Wildling
Space: talk and heal   N: wait for night or morning   O: change your outfit   X: team   Z: Wilddex

Red marks on the ground show where an attack will land: walk away and your Wildling follows you out of danger. Cafes (green cross) heal your team.
