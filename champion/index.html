Champion of the Wildlings

Design your trainer, arrive in Crownhaven by train and choose your first Wildling. Then climb the League from Rank 100 all the way to Champion. There are 60 Wildlings of twelve types to catch, with legendary and mythical ones for the best trainers. The city is full of people out walking and cars driving by.

W, A, S, D: walk (your Wildling follows, so move to dodge!)   Shift: run
Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look around   Mouse wheel: zoom
1, 2, 3: attacks   E: throw a capture orb   F: Starburst   Q: swap Wildling
Space: talk, say hello, heal and sell star shards   B: buy orbs at a shop   N: wait for night or morning
M: map   O: outfit   X: team   Z: Wilddex   T: type chart

Star shards glitter on the streets: pick them up, sell them at a shop (blue sign) and buy capture orbs. Cafes (green cross) heal your team. Red marks on the ground show where an attack will land, so walk away from them.
