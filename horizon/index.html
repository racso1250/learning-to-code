Wild Horizon

A whole island to explore, on foot or on the back of your ride, Zephyrun. Catch 57 brand-new Wildlings in real-time battles, win five Trial Badges, wake the giant Elders to teach Zephyrun to swim and climb, and track down the legendary Wildlings.

W, A, S, D: walk (your Wildling follows, so move to dodge!)   Shift: run   R: ride Zephyrun
Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look around   Mouse wheel: zoom
1, 2, 3: attacks   E: throw a capture orb   F: Starburst   Q: swap Wildling
Space: use doors and wake Elders   M: map   O: outfit   X: team   Z: Wilddex

Follow the yellow diamond on the map. Red marks on the ground show where an attack will land: move away and your Wildling follows. Healing houses (pink roofs) heal your team; shops (blue roofs) sell capture orbs.
