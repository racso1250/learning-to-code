Dino Island 100

You are the new boss of a dinosaur park, and it is already open. But a Velociraptor is out, a fence is nearly broken, and the Triceratops are hungry! Get the park under control. Then send expeditions across a huge island of eight regions to discover over 100 dinosaurs, sea reptiles and flying reptiles, and mix them in the lab to make 22 hybrids. Build lagoons, aviaries, a raptor den, museums, and a giant vault for the most dangerous hybrid of all. Can you fill every page of your Dinodex?

Mouse: point and click. Hold and drag for paddocks and paths. Right click: stop building
W, A, S, D or arrow keys: move the camera   Q, E: turn it   Mouse wheel or Z, X: zoom
Click the little map to jump across the island   B: Dinodex   1: Look   0: Bulldozer   P: pause   O: fast
J: jump in the ranger jeep. Then W, A, S, D to drive and Space to fire a sleepy dart

Choose Look and click a paddock, the Expedition camp or the Research lab to see inside. Rare dinosaurs can only be found once your park has more stars. Every dinosaur has a region it likes best, and visitors can only cross this huge island by monorail, so build stations. The yellow dots on the map are fossil sites: drive the jeep to one to dig up a new dinosaur. Sea reptiles need a Lagoon and flying reptiles need an Aviary. Wild Pteranodons sometimes attack the monorail: send a ranger, drive the jeep to the train, or research the Monorail horn. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
