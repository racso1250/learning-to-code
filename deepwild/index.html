Deepwild

You live in a little floating pod in the middle of the sea. Dive down and explore ten parts of the sea, from sunny shallows to the Abyss. Watch your air, make better gear, build your own base on the sea bed, survive the dark nights, scan the creatures, steer clear of the leviathans, and find the secret at the bottom of the sea.

Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look   W, A, S, D: swim
Space: swim up   C: swim down   Shift: zoom (with the Zipfin scooter)
The rooms of your base have solid walls: press E at a room's round hatch to go in or out
Every room has a fabricator on its wall, by the hatch. It makes gear, supplies, and base pieces such as solar panels
Rooms start empty: go inside and press B to build a locker, a bed, an oven and more. A base needs power from solar panels
Inside your base you stand and walk: W, A, S, D walk, Shift runs, Space jumps (or climbs a lookout's ladder)
1 to 5 or mouse wheel: choose a tool (Hands, Scanner, Net, Builder, Knife)   Left click: use it
E: pick up, use, climb   T: torch   J: sea journal
G: eat a cooked fish   V: drink water   H: first aid kit
B: build your base   X: pack a piece up   R: sea whistle

Air runs out under water: swim up to the top to breathe, or climb into your pod (E at the ladder). Every upgrade lets you go further and deeper. If you run out of air you wake up in your pod, but the sea keeps half of what you were carrying (things in your pod locker are safe) and your count of days survived starts again.
