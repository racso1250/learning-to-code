Deepwild

You live on a little raft in the middle of the sea. Dive down and explore: sunny shallows, a kelp forest, coral gardens, a twilight slope and a dark trench. Watch your air, make better gear, scan the creatures, and find the secret at the bottom of the sea.

Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look   W, A, S, D: swim
Space: swim up   C: swim down   Shift: zoom (with the Zipfin scooter)
E: pick up, use, climb   F: torch   Right mouse button or Q: scan a creature   J: sea journal

Air runs out under water: swim up to the top to breathe, or climb onto your raft (E at the ladder). Every upgrade lets you go further and deeper. If you run out of air you wake up on the raft, but the sea keeps half of what you were carrying.
