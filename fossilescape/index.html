Fossil Bay: Lights Out

You are on holiday at the Fossil Bay dinosaur park. In the night the power fails and every dinosaur gets out. Everybody is rushed to the boats. Everybody except you: you slept right through the sirens. Now it is morning, and you are alone on the island. Find the car the rangers left behind, and get away.

Mouse (click the game first) or arrow keys: look around   W, A, S, D: walk
Hold C: creep quietly   Hold Shift: run (loud!)   E: use, search, hide
Later on: click or Space to fire a sleepy dart   G: throw a flare
In the car: W, A, S, D to drive   E: get out

Hunters notice noise. Creeping is quiet, walking is louder, running and driving are very loud, and standing still is silent. They also see further in front of their noses, so pass behind them. A T. rex can only see things that move. If a hunter is after you, get inside a building (E at a door) and wait. If you are caught, you start again from the last safe place and keep everything you have found. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address.
