Fossil Bay: Wild Again

Fossil Bay was once a dinosaur park. It closed years ago, and nobody has been back since. Now you are leading an expedition to find out what lives there. Take your boat to the island, scan every kind of creature you can find, and bring back the three lost data drives of the old park. They say the park's lab made things it never told anyone about...

W, A, S, D: walk, or steer the boat   Mouse or arrow keys: look   Shift: run   C: sneak and duck
E: get in and out of the boat, climb, open, take   Hold Space (or the mouse button): scan
G: throw a flare   Q: use a medkit   B: field guide

Nothing can reach you at the top of a watchtower, and they are good places to scan from. Meat-eaters can't resist going to look at a burning flare. The T. rex can't see you if you stand perfectly still. Land animals won't follow you into deep water, but something else lives out there, and it turns slowly: keep your speed up and steer hard. The blue beams of light mark the data drives. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
