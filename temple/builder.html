The Great Land

Welcome to Free Play! This massive land is peaceful: nothing can hurt you here.

Gather materials, fix the old ruins, and build villages. Build a boat to sail to the islands!

The more people live in your land, the higher your rank. Every new rank gives you a new costume and a new weapon.

Arrows / WASD: walk and gather   B: build   Space: fix ruins and talk

Pick a building below, face an empty patch of land and press B to build it. Walk up to a ruin and press Space to fix it.
