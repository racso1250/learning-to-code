Trenchlight

You are the pilot of the Lantern, a little submarine in the dark sea under the ice of a frozen moon. The Lift back to the surface has lost its four power cores. Each one fell into the home of a leviathan. Drill for crystals and metal, build your own bases on the sea bed, make your sub stronger, and bring all four cores home.

W: go forward   S: go back   A, D or mouse (click the game first): turn
Arrow up and down or mouse: point the nose up and down
Space: rise   C: sink   Shift: boost
Hold E: drill, or grab a power core   E: park at a dock
L: lights   Q: sonar ping   G: glow decoy   R: shock pulse
B: build your base   X: pack a piece up   M: map

Every layer of the crater is deeper than the last, and your hull can only take so much. Build a workshop and a turbine near a dock, then park at the dock to make your sub stronger. If your hull breaks, the sub's safety bubble floats you back to your last dock, but you lose the cargo you were carrying. Things in your store room are always safe.
