Clover Cove

You have just moved to a tiny village by the sea. Shake trees for fruit, catch bugs and fish, dig up fossils for the museum, make friends with your animal neighbours, and fill your house with furniture. There is no hurry and no way to lose.

1, 2, 3, 4: hands, net, rod, shovel   Space: use your tool   B: pockets   J: journal

Bugs fly away if you run at them, so walk. Fish nibble a few times first: only press Space when the float goes right under. Different bugs and fish come out in each season, and some only at night. A day lasts ten minutes, and sleeping in your bed skips to morning. Your journal (J) gives hints about what you have not found yet. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
