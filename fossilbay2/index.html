Fossil Bay 2

A whole island is yours: jungle, desert, swamp and snow, cut apart by two rivers. Build monorail stations to open each biome. Hatch 25 kinds of dinosaur, sea reptile and flying reptile. Build research labs to find new DNA, and hire keepers, rangers, scientists and tour guides to help you run it all.

Mouse: point and click to build. Hold and drag for paddocks and paths. Right click: stop building
W, A, S, D or arrow keys: move the camera   Q, E: turn it   Mouse wheel or Z, X: zoom
Click the little map to jump across the island   1: Look   0: Bulldozer   P: pause   O: fast
J: jump in the ranger jeep. Then W, A, S, D to drive and Space to fire a sleepy dart

Paths can't cross the rivers, so each biome needs its own monorail station before you can build there. Every dinosaur has a biome it likes best. New kinds have to be found by a research lab, and a lab needs a scientist. Sea reptiles live in lagoons and flying reptiles in aviaries. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
