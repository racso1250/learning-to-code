Wildlings

151 wild creatures live across a huge land. Pick your first Wildling, then explore, battle, catch and evolve them. Beat the Guardians to earn their badges. Can you catch all 151?

Arrows / WASD: move and pick   Space: choose, talk, pick berries   Shift: run   X: your team   Z: the Wilddex

Walk into a wild creature to battle it. Wear it down, then throw an orb to catch it. Berry bushes and villagers heal your team.
