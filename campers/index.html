Camp Fossil Bay

It was meant to be the best summer camp ever: a week on an island with real dinosaurs. Then the storm came, the fences went down, and the ferry left without you. Find your five friends, use what each of them is good at, and cross the island to the radio on the hill. And look after Bonk.

W, A, S, D or arrow keys: walk   Shift: run   E: do things
1 to 6 (or Tab): swap to another camper   Q: use their skill   G: throw a flare

Each camper can do one thing the others can't, so swap between them with the number keys. Long grass hides you from raptors: stay out of their red view cones, and don't run near them. Campfires give all your hearts back and save the game. If you run out of hearts, everybody just runs back to the last campfire. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
