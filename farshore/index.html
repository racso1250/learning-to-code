Farshore

Your landing pod has come down on a far-away world of islands and warm seas. Walk the beaches, climb the hills and dive under the waves. Build your own base from room blocks, on land or under the sea. Find the five star stones to wake the old signal tower. And watch out: three leviathans live here too.

Mouse (click the game first) or arrow keys: look around   W, A, S, D: walk or swim
Space: jump, swim up, climb a ladder   C: swim down, climb down   Shift: run
E: pick up, use, go through a door   H: eat a sunfruit
B: build   X: pack up a room or furniture   L: dive light   R: sea horn

Your air runs out under water: swim up to the top to breathe, or go inside a room of your base (rooms are always full of air, even on the sea bed). Rooms that touch join up, and every room has a ladder to the room above or to the roof. Build a workbench in a room to make flippers, bigger air tanks and more. If you are badly hurt you wake up in your bed, and you keep all your things.
