Fossil Bay 3

This time you are down on the ground. Walk the island's five sections: the Home Meadow, the Greenwood Forest, the Red Desert, the Frost Plateau and the Misty Swamp. Put wild dinosaurs to sleep with your dart gun, carry them home under your helicopter, and build the park around them by hand.

Mouse (click the game first) or arrow keys: look around   W, A, S, D: walk   Shift: run   C: creep
Click or Space: fire a sleepy dart   E: use (helicopter, darts crate, check a paddock)
B: build. Then look at the ground and click. Esc: stop building
In the helicopter: W, A, S, D to fly   Space: up   C: down   E: hook, lower, get out

Bigger dinosaurs need more darts. Plant-eaters run when they are hit; meat-eaters come straight for you. A sleeping dinosaur shows on the little map. Fly low over it and press E to hook it, then fly over a paddock and press E to lower it in. Every kind likes the section it came from best. The forest is too thick to land in: land at its edge, or clear trees with the Bulldozer. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address.
