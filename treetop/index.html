Treetop Island

You live in a giant tree, in a forest, on an island full of dinosaurs. First you design your treehouse. Then you climb down into the forest to find food, make tools, and build on new parts. Watch out for raptors and the T. rex, and be back in your hammock before dark!

W, A, S, D: walk   Mouse or arrow keys: look   Shift: run   C: sneak
E: pick up, climb, chop, use   Q: eat   Space or click: jab with your spear
T: torch   H: horn (when you have built them)

Nothing can reach you up in your treehouse. Raptors hate deep water, small dinosaurs are scared of fire, and the T. rex can't see you if you stand perfectly still. Triceratops and Stegosaurus only want to be left alone: a "?" over their head means back away. If the game runs slowly, add ?plain to the end of the address to switch the shadows off.
