A first-person forest horror

Nightwood

You are lost in a forest that does not want you to leave. Find the six rune keys to open the gate. Keep your campfire burning at night: the things in the trees are afraid of fire.

Mouse (or left and right arrows): look   WASD: move   Shift: run
Space or click: flash your torch at a monster   E: feed the fire   P: pause

The yellow arrow always points back to your camp. Monsters will not come into the firelight. Some freeze while you look at them. Others hide behind trees and jump out.
