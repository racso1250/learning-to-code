Ra Ra Escape

One night you hear a strange noise: ra... ra... ra... It is a stick bug. Then it grows to the size of a bus, and it chases you! Run through the City, the Swamp, the Mountains and the Alaskan Redwood Forest, all the way to the evil scientist who made it.

W, A, S, D: run   Mouse (click the game first) or left and right arrows: look round
Shift: sprint   Space: jump   E: hide, push, pull, use

The bar at the top shows how close the stick bug is. In the City, look at what a building is made of before you hide in it: stone and brick are strong, glass and thin wood are not. In the Swamp, stay on the boards and hide in the reeds. In the Mountains, jump the cracks and push boulders. If it catches you, you start again from the last safe spot.
