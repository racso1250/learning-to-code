BEASTLINGS

Design your character, explore the Kova region, catch all 68 Beastlings, win five gym badges and beat the Champion.

POCKET BEAST COLOR

Arrows or WASD: walk   Z or Space: A button (talk, choose)   X: B button (back, hold to run)   Enter: menu (save, team, Dex, and LOOK to change your character)

Wild Beastlings hide in tall grass. Weaken one, then throw a Snare Orb. Walk into a door to go inside, talk to the person there with Z, and leave by the red doormat. A + building heals your team; a $ building is a shop.
