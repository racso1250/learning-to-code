Rings of Power

You are Galadriel. Five rings of power were stolen and hidden across the land, each guarded by a giant, and the last by Sauron himself. Win them back one by one. Every ring gives you a new power, and opens the way to the next.

Arrows / WASD: move   Space: sword, talk, pick berries   X: use your ring's power   1 to 5: choose a ring   P: pause

Each gate between the lands only opens to one ring's power: stand in front of it, choose the right ring and press X.
