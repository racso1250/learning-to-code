Wildlings: Thunder Ride

Thunder Ride

Ride Voltrev, the motorbike Wildling, down the long road through five different places. Chase and catch wild Wildlings, hop off to battle, and stop the Rust Riders gang.

Riding: Up (or W) go   Down (or S) brake   Left and right steer   Shift: boost   E: throw a capture orb   R: hop off
On foot: W A S D walk   Left and right (or the mouse): look   1, 2, 3 attack   Q: swap   F: Overdrive
Space: talk and use pit stops   X: your team

Wild Wildlings run away from the bike: chase them down and throw orbs with E. On foot, red marks on the ground show where an attack will land, so step away and your Wildling follows. Only Voltrev can go into Overdrive.
