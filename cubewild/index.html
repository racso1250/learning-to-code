Cubewild

A whole world made of blocks, and it goes on forever. Dig, build, craft and explore. Make friends with the animals, and light lanterns before the monsters come out at night. Craft a sword, a hammer or a bow to fight them off!

Mouse: look   WASD: move   Space: jump   Shift: run
Left click: dig, or strike with a weapon   Right click: build, use, pet   1 to 9 or wheel: pick a block
E: backpack and crafting   Esc: stop
Creative mode only: tap Space twice (or G) to fly   T: change the time of day

Explorer: dig for your blocks and watch your hearts. Creative: every block is free, nothing hurts you, and you can fly.

Explorer mode: collect blocks by digging, and watch your hearts. Creative mode: every block for free, no danger, and you can fly. You can swap any time with the Mode button.
